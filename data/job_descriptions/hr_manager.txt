Job Title: HR Manager
Location: Chennai, Tamil Nadu

Overview
Our growing manufacturing unit is looking for an experienced HR Manager to lead recruitment and employee relations for a 250+ person team.

Qualifications
- 5+ years of experience in Human Resources, with at least 2 years in a managerial role
- Experience with HRIS systems (Darwinbox, SAP SuccessFactors, or equivalent)
- MBA in HR or a Master's degree in a related field
- Strong understanding of labour law compliance

Responsibilities
- Lead end-to-end recruitment for multiple departments
- Design and improve onboarding processes
- Handle employee grievances and disciplinary matters
- Partner with leadership on workforce planning
